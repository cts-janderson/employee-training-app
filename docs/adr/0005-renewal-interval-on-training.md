# Renewal interval belongs to the training, and administrators start renewals

A training may carry a renewal interval in whole months. A completion's renewal date is its completion date in the organization time zone plus that interval, clamped to the end of the month. The interval belongs to the logical training rather than a published version, so publishing a revision cannot silently change an employee's cycle. This is deliberately the smallest stored policy: one number per training, not the configurable policy builder the [implementation plan](../implementation-plan.md#deferred-scope) defers. The alternative, a cutoff date entered at each review, left employees without a renewal date to see and made the cycle depend on administrators remembering it.

Renewals are created only when an administrator reviews and submits them. They are never queued automatically. This keeps the story's review-before-creation rule and avoids the deferred automatic reassignment. Each renewal is a new assignment that records the completed assignment it renews and its cycle number. Earlier evidence is never altered, and [ADR-0001](0001-one-outstanding-assignment-per-training.md) conflicts are reported for administrator action.
