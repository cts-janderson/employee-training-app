# Waivers are final and recorded by any other administrator

Any administrator may waive an outstanding assignment with a reason, except one that belongs to them. The recorded actor, reason and decision time provide accountability without the separate approver role and request-and-approve flow that the deferred permission hierarchies would require. Blocking self-waivers costs one check and removes the obvious conflict of interest.

A waiver is final. It ends the obligation as a distinct outcome, like completion and cancellation, and preserves the version, due date, acknowledgments and attempts. If the requirement must apply again, an administrator creates a new assignment. Revocation that reopens the original assignment was rejected for now. It would need rules for a deadline that has passed, for rescheduling reminders, and for a newer outstanding assignment that [ADR-0001](0001-one-outstanding-assignment-per-training.md) forbids alongside the reopened one.
